# Launch film: three script options

Written for someone who has never heard of Polyxd. Each option carries one idea and nothing else:

> Apps only have the screens someone built in advance. Polyxd lets AI build the screen you need, on the spot, in the app's own design, and checks it works.

Rules for all three:

- One thing moves at a time. A transition finishes before anything else starts.
- At most one line of text on screen, and often none. Every line holds long enough to read twice.
- Shots last 4 to 10 seconds. Total length 40 to 50 seconds.
- Left out on purpose: JSON, tokens, thirteen design systems, the verifier grid, accessibility trees, agents, Studio, the shell, renderers, React and Web Components. Those belong on the website, for people who are already interested.

---

## Option A · "The ask"

A single person, a single moment. Emotional and simple. About 46 seconds.

| Time | Picture | Words on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–6 | A phone with a banking app. A thumb types "Split dinner with Priya, £40". The app replies: "Sorry, I can't help with that." Hold on it. | none |
| 6–11 | Plain paper. | Apps only have the screens someone built in advance. |
| 11–15 | Back to the phone. The same words typed again. | none |
| 15–25 | This time a screen assembles, slowly: Priya, £40, a Send button. The thumb taps Send. "Sent." | Polyxd lets AI build the screen you asked for. |
| 25–35 | The same screen changes into two other apps' styles, one at a time, each held for a beat. | In your app's own design. |
| 35–40 | A small tick lands beside the screen. | Checked before anyone sees it. |
| 40–46 | The Polyxd mark. It blinks once. | polyxd.com |

**Why it works:** everyone has hit "Sorry, I can't help with that". The second attempt is the story.

## Option B · "Show, don't tell" (recommended)

A side-by-side comparison. It explains *why* in one glance. About 42 seconds.

| Time | Picture | Words on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–5 | A shopping app's assistant. Someone asks "Can I return these shoes?" | none |
| 5–12 | The assistant answers with a long paragraph of instructions: go to Orders, find the item, tap Return, choose a reason… The paragraph keeps scrolling. | none |
| 12–16 | Plain paper. | AI can answer anything. But it answers in paragraphs. |
| 16–26 | The same question. This time the answer is a real screen: the shoes, a reason picker, a "Start return" button. A tap, and "Return started". | Polyxd turns the answer into a screen. |
| 26–34 | Two more apps, one at a time, four seconds each. A bank: "Send Priya £40" becomes a payment screen. A council service: "I've moved" becomes an address form. Each looks like its own app. | Any app. In its own design. |
| 34–38 | A small tick lands beside the last screen. | Checked before anyone sees it. |
| 38–42 | The Polyxd mark. It blinks once. | polyxd.com |

**Why it's recommended:** a stranger sees the problem and the answer side by side within twelve seconds, without needing to know what a design system is.

## Option C · "How it works"

A calm explainer, built from simple diagrams. Best for a developer audience. About 50 seconds.

| Time | Picture | Words on screen |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7 | A simple payment screen, centred. | This is a screen. |
| 7–15 | The screen fades into a plain list of its parts: "Choose a person", "Amount", "Send button". | Polyxd describes it as a list of parts. |
| 15–23 | A question is typed above the list, and the list writes itself underneath. | So an AI can write one. |
| 23–33 | The list turns back into the screen, in a bank's style. Then again, in a shop's style. | Your app draws it in its own design. |
| 33–41 | A tick lands beside the screen. | And checks it works for everyone. |
| 41–50 | The Polyxd mark. It blinks once. | polyxd.com · open source |

**Why it works:** it answers "what is it actually?" for people who will build with it. It is the least emotional of the three.

## Music, for whichever option is chosen

The synthesised track is gone. Two ways forward:

- **A licensed track** from a library such as Artlist, Musicbed or Epidemic Sound. Search for "minimal piano, hopeful, 90 bpm" or "soft pulse, tech, understated". This gives the best result and needs your account.
- **GarageBand's Apple Loops,** which are royalty-free. GarageBand has to stay in front while the loops are assembled, which failed from this session. It works if you click into GarageBand when asked.

Sound effects stay minimal: a soft tap when the thumb touches the screen and a single tick at the end.
