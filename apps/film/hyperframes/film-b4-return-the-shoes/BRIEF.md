## Intent

Three short launch films for Polyxd, one per script option in `apps/film/SCRIPT-OPTIONS.md`:
A "The ask" (~46 s), B "Show, don't tell" (~42-52 s), C "How it works" (~42-50 s).
Written for a stranger. One idea per film.

The owner rejected an earlier cut: "no storytelling, too much text on screen at all times, I can't
focus, I can't make out what's happening, things are so fast, transitions happening at the same time;
music is very bad." So:

- One thing moves at a time; a transition finishes before anything else starts.
- At most one short line of text on screen, often none; every line holds long enough to read twice.
- Shots 4-10 s. Calm pace. Nothing bounces.
- Leave out JSON, tokens, verifier grids, Studio, renderers.
